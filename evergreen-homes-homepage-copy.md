## Voice and Tone Notes

- **Formality:** Professional but warm — like a knowledgeable friend, not a brochure
- **Personality:** Confident and grounded — no hype, no exclamation points
- **Key word choices:** "Jon" (not "our team"), "your home" (not "the home"), "every day" (not "regularly")
- **What to avoid:** Corporate language ("leverage," "solutions"), vague promises ("world-class"), stacked metaphors
