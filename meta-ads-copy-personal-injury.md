## Testing Strategy (Per Paid-Ads Skill Framework)

**Priority order for testing:**
1. **Hook/angle** — Test Variation A vs. B first (PAS vs. Question or BAB). This has the highest impact on CTR.
2. **Headline** — Once winning primary text is found, test headline variations.
3. **Description** — Test last, as it has the least impact on Meta.

**Recommended approach:**
- Run 2-3 primary text variations per ad in the same ad set
- Let Meta's algorithm optimize delivery for 3-5 days before making decisions
- Kill underperformers based on CTR and cost-per-lead, not impressions
- Scale winners by duplicating into new ad sets with broader audiences

## Psychological Principles Applied (Per Marketing Psychology Skill)

| Principle | How It's Used |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Loss Aversion** | "Don't let wrong info hurt your case," "You could lose thousands" |
| **Authority Bias** | "Decades of experience," "Top-rated," attorney credentials |
| **Social Proof** | "Millions recovered," "Thousands of clients helped" |
| **Reciprocity** | Free case review / free consultation — give value first |
| **Scarcity/Urgency** | "Don't wait," "Before the insurance company builds its case" |
| **Status-Quo Bias** | "No upfront cost" removes friction to take action |
| **Zero Risk** | "No fees unless we win" eliminates financial barrier |
