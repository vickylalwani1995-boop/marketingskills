## Part A: Quick Wins (Do These Today)

### 1. Rewrite Your Headline (Skill: `copywriting`, `page-cro`)

**The problem:**
"Your Trusted White-Label eCommerce & Digital Marketing Partner" describes *what you are*, but doesn't tell an agency *why they should care*. A visitor should understand your value in under 5 seconds.

**What to change — pick one of these:**

| Option | Headline | Why It Works |
|--------|----------|--------------|
| **A (Recommended)** | **Scale Your Agency Without Hiring a Single Developer** | Speaks to the #1 pain point — agencies want to grow without overhead. Outcome-focused. |
| B | **White-Label eCommerce Teams That Make Your Agency Look Good** | Highlights the white-label benefit (their clients never know). Feels like a partnership. |
| C | **Stop Turning Down Projects. Start Scaling Your Agency.** | Creates urgency. Agencies hate saying "no" to clients. Two punchy sentences. |

**Subheadline (add beneath the headline):**
> We handle Shopify, Magento, and WooCommerce development + digital marketing so your agency can take on more clients without growing your team.

This one sentence covers: what you do, which platforms, and the benefit. Plain English, no jargon.

### 2. Fix Your Button Text (Skill: `copywriting`, `page-cro`)

**The problem:**
"Let's Work Together" / "Get Started" / "Contact Us" are vague. The visitor doesn't know what happens when they click. Vague buttons get fewer clicks.

**What to change:**

| Instead of | Use this | Why |
|------------|----------|-----|
| "Contact Us" | **Book a Free Discovery Call** | Tells them exactly what happens next. "Free" removes risk. |
| "Get Started" | **See How We Work With Agencies** | Lower commitment for visitors still researching. Good as a secondary button. |
| "Let's Work Together" | **Talk to Our Team — It's Free** | Friendly, removes the "will they try to sell me?" worry. |

**Rule of thumb:** Your button should finish the sentence "I want to ___."
- "I want to *book a free discovery call*" — clear.
- "I want to *get started*" — started with what?

### 3. Make Your Numbers Impossible to Miss (Skill: `page-cro`)

**The problem:**
You have great proof (14+ years, multi-platform expertise) but it's probably buried in paragraphs. Agencies scanning your page will skip right past it.

**What to change:**
Add a bold stats bar near the top of the page (just below your hero section). Simple layout, big numbers:

```
[ 14+ Years Experience ]  [ 3 Platforms: Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce ]  [ Agencies in 3 Countries ]
```

If you can add these numbers, even better:
- "**X+** projects delivered"
- "**X** agency partners and counting"
- "**100%** white-label — your clients never know"

**Why:** Numbers are the fastest way to build trust. People scan numbers before they read sentences.

### 4. Add a Trust Line Next to Your Contact Form (Skill: `form-cro`)

**The problem:**
Agency owners are cautious. They won't fill out a form if they think they'll get spammed or hard-sold.

**What to add — right next to or below your form's submit button:**
> "No spam. No hard sell. Just a 15-minute chat to see if we're a fit."

Also add: **expected response time** (e.g., "We'll respond within 4 business hours").

These tiny additions reduce the "what happens after I submit?" anxiety.

### 5. Add a Clear "Who This Is For" Line (Skill: `page-cro`, `copywriting`)

**The problem:**
If an agency lands on your homepage and isn't sure this is *for them specifically*, they'll leave. Generic service pages lose to specific ones.

**What to add (near the top, under your subheadline):**
> **Built for agencies** that want to scale eCommerce and digital marketing delivery — without the cost and risk of hiring in-house.

Or even simpler:
> We work exclusively with digital agencies in the USA, UK, and Australia.

**Why:** When a visitor from an agency reads "we work exclusively with agencies," they immediately think: *"Oh, this is for me."* That's half the battle.

## Part B: Bigger Changes (Do These This Week)

### 6. Add a "How It Works" Section — 3 Steps (Skill: `page-cro`, `copywriting`)

**The problem:**
Agencies considering outsourcing worry: *"Will this be complicated? Will I lose control? Will the quality be bad?"* If your page doesn't answer this, they won't book a call.

**What to add — a simple 3-step section:**

> **How It Works**
>
> **Step 1: Book a Discovery Call**
> Tell us about your agency, your clients, and the type of projects you need help with.
>
> **Step 2: We Handle the Work**
> Our team builds, launches, and manages the project — fully white-label, under your brand.
>
> **Step 3: You Deliver and Scale**
> You deliver great work to your clients, grow your revenue, and take on more projects.

**Why:** Three steps make anything feel simple. It answers "what's the process?" without the visitor having to guess.

### 8. Add an Objection-Handling FAQ Section (Skill: `page-cro`)

**The problem:**
Agency owners have specific worries about outsourcing. If your page doesn't address them, visitors leave to "think about it" and never come back.

**What to add — an FAQ section with these 4-5 questions:**

| Question | Answer Approach |
|----------|----------------|
| "Will my clients know I'm outsourcing?" | No — explain your 100% white-label process. |
| "What if the quality isn't good enough?" | Explain your QA process, revision policy, or trial project option. |
| "How do you communicate and manage projects?" | Name your tools (Slack, Asana, etc.) and response times. |
| "What's the minimum commitment?" | If there's no lock-in, say so. Agencies hate contracts. |
| "How fast can you start?" | Give a real timeline. Speed wins deals. |

**Why this matters:** Every unanswered question is a reason NOT to book a call. FAQs do the selling for you while you sleep.

### 9. Set Up Basic Conversion Tracking (Skill: `analytics-tracking`)

**The problem:**
You said conversions feel low, but without tracking you can't know *where* visitors drop off or *which* changes helped.

**What to set up (ask your web person to help — it takes about 30 minutes):**

1. **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** — if not already installed
2. **Track these 3 events:**
   - `cta_clicked` — when someone clicks "Book a Discovery Call"
   - `form_submitted` — when the contact/booking form is submitted
   - `page_scrolled` — how far people scroll (GA4 does this automatically)
3. **Mark `form_submitted` as a Conversion** in GA4 settings

**Why:** Once this is live, you'll know your actual conversion rate. After you make the changes above, you'll be able to *see* whether they worked — not just guess.

### 10. Reduce Your Form to the Essentials (Skill: `form-cro`)

**The problem:**
Every extra field on a form costs you submissions. Each field you add typically reduces completion by 10-25%.

**What to do — for your contact/discovery call form, only ask:**

| Field | Required? | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Name | Yes | To greet them properly |
| Work Email | Yes | To respond and verify they're from an agency |
| Agency Website | Optional | Lets you do homework before the call |
| "What do you need help with?" (dropdown) | Optional | Helps you prep, not block |

**Remove (if present):** phone number (ask on the call), company size, budget range, detailed message box.

**Button text:** "Book My Free Discovery Call"
