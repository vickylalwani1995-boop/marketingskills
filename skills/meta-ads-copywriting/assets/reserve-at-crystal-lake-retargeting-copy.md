## Copy Guidance Notes

### Retargeting Strategy
- These ads target audiences who have already engaged with The Reserve at Crystal Lake — they know the brand. Do not introduce the development from scratch.
- Rotate all three variations in the same ad set to prevent creative fatigue and test which angle resonates best.
- Suggested retargeting windows: 1–30 days for Variation 1 (urgency), 7–60 days for Variation 2 (social proof), 14–90 days for Variation 3 (aspiration).

### A/B Testing Priority
1. Hook (first line) — test scarcity vs. social proof vs. pain point
2. CTA button — "Explore Homes" vs. "Book Your Tour" vs. "Request Information"
3. Headline — urgency-led vs. benefit-led
4. Primary text length — full version vs. trimmed version (first 2 paragraphs only)

### Placement Notes
- Facebook Feed: All variations work — primary text is shown above image
- Instagram Feed: Lead with hook; first 2 lines must work standalone (copy gets truncated)
- Instagram Stories: Use headline + CTA only — keep it tight
- Facebook Marketplace (if applicable): Emphasize availability and specifics
