## Creative Direction Notes

### Image Recommendations
- Calm, warm imagery (soft lighting, natural tones, greenery)
- Diverse representation reflecting Oakland County community
- Avoid clinical/sterile settings — show warmth and comfort
- Human faces (real, not stock) preferred
- Keep text overlay under 20% of image area

### Video Ad Structure (15-30 sec)
1. **Hook (0-3 sec):** Bold question or relatable pain point
2. **Problem (3-8 sec):** Describe what the viewer is feeling
3. **Solution (8-20 sec):** Introduce Tranquil Awakenings and their approach
4. **CTA (20-30 sec):** "Book your free consultation today"

### Production Tips
- Always include captions (85% of Facebook users watch without sound)
- Vertical format for Stories/Reels, square for feed
- Native, authentic feel outperforms overly polished production
- Use calm background music (wellness/meditation tone)

## Budget Allocation Recommendation

### Testing Phase (First 2-4 weeks)
| Campaign | % of Budget | Rationale |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Individual Therapy | 40% | Broadest audience, highest volume |
| Couples Therapy | 20% | Strong demand, high-value service |
| Family Therapy | 15% | High lifetime value, multi-session service |
| Psychological Testing | 15% | Niche but high intent |
| Retargeting | 10% | Re-engage warm traffic |

### Optimization Triggers
- **If CPA is too high:** Tighten geographic targeting, test new creative angles
- **If CTR is low:** Test new hooks, swap creative, refresh copy angles
- **If CPM is high:** Broaden audience slightly, try different placements
- Move budget toward the best-performing ad sets after 2 weeks of data

## Psychology Principles Applied

| Principle | Where Applied |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Loss Aversion** | Framing what they'll lose by not acting ("anxiety doesn't have to run your life") |
| **Social Proof** | Client testimonials in multiple ads |
| **Zero-Price Effect** | "Free consultation" prominently featured in every ad |
| **Reciprocity** | Offering free value first (consultation) before asking for commitment |
| **Status-Quo Bias** | Reducing friction to change ("no pressure, no commitment") |
| **Commitment & Consistency** | Small first step (free consultation) leads to therapy commitment |
| **Authority Bias** | "Evidence-based," specific therapy modalities named |
| **Contrast Effect** | Before/after framing in BAB ads |
| **Regret Aversion** | "No commitment" and "no pressure" language reduces fear of regret |
| **Mere Exposure Effect** | Retargeting campaigns build familiarity over time |
